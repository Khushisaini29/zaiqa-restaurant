Our story

Twenty-eight years, two tandoors and one stubborn belief — great Indian food cannot be rushed.

began
Since 1998

From a six-table dhaba to Rohtak's dining room

Zaiqa began as a roadside dhaba where truckers on NH-9 would queue for our hand-pounded butter chicken. Word travelled faster than the trucks. Within a decade, the dhaba became a dining hall; within two, a name people drove three hours to taste.

What never changed: the river-clay lining of our tandoors, the 4 AM stone-grinding of masalas, and the rule that nothing leaves the pass unless the chef would serve it to his own mother.

“We don't plate food at Zaiqa. We plate the afternoons our grandmothers spent over slow fires.”

What We Stand For

Three fires that never go out

River-Clay Tandoor

Our tandoors are re-lined by hand every season with clay from the Yamuna banks, and fired only with hardwood charcoal — never gas. You can taste the difference in one bite of naan.

Slow-Cooked by Design

Dal makhani simmers 48 hours. Biryani seals and steams for 40 minutes after you order. Kormas rest overnight so the spice settles. The clock is our busiest kitchen tool.

Atithi Devo Bhava

“The guest is god” isn't a slogan on our wall — it's why we remember your corner table, call before your anniversary, and never let your glass reach empty.

At the Pass

Chef Vikram Rathore

Trained in the royal kitchens of Jaipur and sharpened in two Michelin-starred houses abroad, Chef Vikram came home in 2014 with one mission: prove that dhaba soul and fine-dining craft belong on the same plate.

His tasting counter seats six guests a night, elbow-to-elbow with the tandoor — the hottest reservation in Haryana.

The Journey

Milestones along the highway

'98

The dhaba years

Six tables off NH-9. Hand-pounded spices, kerosene lamps, and a butter chicken that stopped trucks.

'07

First dining hall

Forty covers in Rohtak city. The first river-clay tandoor is installed — it still fires today.

'16

The tandoor studio

An open kitchen and chef's counter. Chef Vikram's team starts documenting 200+ family recipes.

'26

Zaiqa, reimagined

Our new avatar — the brass room, the copper bar, and now, your table bookable in thirty seconds.
