Our Story

A love letter to Indian cuisine

Born in 1998 as a six-table dhaba on Delhi Road, Zaiqa grew on one stubborn belief — that great Indian food cannot be rushed. Our tandoors are still lined with river clay, our masalas still stone-ground at dawn, and our dal still ignores the clock.

Today, Chef Vikram Rathore's kitchen plates that same dhaba soul with fine-dining grace.

  • River-clay tandoors, live charcoal
  • Masalas stone-ground daily
  • Pampore saffron, aged basmati
  • Farm produce within 60 km
